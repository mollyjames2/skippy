Daylight uses local sunrise/sunset and rounds to full hours.
Environment
Coastal uses stricter hard-gate thresholds. Estuary/Sheltered relaxes wave-related hard gates for upriver trips.

Fair weather sailor?
Heavily penalise the boating score in rainy conditions. The harder the rain, the bigger the penalty.
