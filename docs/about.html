What Skippy does
Skippy gives boaters a fast, at-a-glance view of upcoming conditions for familiar coastal spots.

It’s designed to answer the first question quickly:
“Is it worth going out (or planning a trip)?”

Skippy combines wind, waves, precipitation, visibility and daylight into a simple Boating Score, with a week view for scanning ahead and a day view for more detail.
Understanding the score
90+
Excellent – great conditions for most boating
60+
Good – generally manageable
40+
OK – more effort, less comfort
20+
Poor – think carefully
<20
Avoid – conditions are unfavourable
The score is a quick guide, not a guarantee. Always check official forecasts and use your judgment.
Factors Skippy considers
Wind: Stronger winds (and gusts) usually mean harder boating.
Waves: Bigger seas reduce comfort and increase risk.
Rain: Less pleasant, and can reduce visibility.
Visibility: Fog / haze can make navigation and lookout harder.
Daylight: Sunrise/sunset helps you plan around light.
Tides
Where available, Skippy shows modelled tide events (high/low) based on the underlying sea level curve.

Treat this as a handy guide rather than a harbour-master source.
Data & privacy
Skippy uses the Open-Meteo weather + marine models. There are no accounts and no tracking built into the app.

Your settings and presets are stored locally in your browser.
Safety notice
Skippy provides guidance only. Always check official marine forecasts, carry appropriate safety equipment, and plan conservatively. Conditions can change rapidly.
