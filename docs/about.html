What Skippy does
Skippy helps recreational boaters in South West England decide when it is safe and enjoyable to go boating.

We analyze wind, waves, tides, visibility, and daylight to produce an overall Boating Score and recommended time windows.
Understanding the ratings
80+
Excellent - Ideal conditions
60+
Good - Suitable for most boats
40+
Fair - Experienced skippers only
<40
Poor / Avoid - Not recommended
Factors we consider
Wind: Strong winds increase difficulty and risk.
Waves: Higher waves reduce comfort and safety.
Tides: Access may be limited around high and low water.
Visibility: Fog and haze increase collision risk.
Safety notice
Skippy provides guidance only. Always check official marine forecasts, carry appropriate safety equipment, and plan conservatively. Conditions can change rapidly.
