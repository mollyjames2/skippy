What Skippy does
Skippy gives boaters a fast, at-a-glance view of upcoming conditions for familiar coastal spots.

It’s designed to answer the first question quickly:
“Is it worth going out (or planning a trip)?”

Skippy combines wind, waves, precipitation, visibility and daylight into a simple Boating Score, with a week view for scanning ahead and a day view for more detail.
Understanding the score
90+
Excellent – great conditions for most boating
60+
Good – generally manageable
40+
OK – more effort, less comfort
20+
Poor – think carefully
<20
Avoid – conditions are unfavourable
The score is a quick guide, not a guarantee. Always check official forecasts and use your judgment.
Hard safety gates
Skippy also applies hard gates for truly unsafe conditions.

If a hard gate triggers, the hour (and any daily score derived from it) is forced to Avoid regardless of the normal score calculation.

These thresholds are intentionally tunable in the source (not in the UI).
Environment: Coastal vs Estuary/Sheltered
Skippy includes a Settings toggle for Environment:
Coastal (default) vs Estuary/Sheltered.

Why? The underlying weather/marine model is relatively coarse and can’t reliably “see” how sheltered conditions upriver can be compared to open water.

When you choose Estuary/Sheltered, Skippy:
• relaxes some hard gates (especially wave-related), and
• reduces how strongly offshore swell/wave inputs dominate the score, to better reflect an upriver trip intent.

This is meant to help you avoid missing genuine sheltered boating opportunities when it’s rough outside but manageable upriver.
Factors Skippy considers
Wind: Stronger winds (and gusts) usually mean harder boating.
Waves: Bigger seas reduce comfort and increase risk (especially offshore).
Rain: Less pleasant, and can reduce visibility.
Visibility: Fog / haze can make navigation and lookout harder.
Daylight: Sunrise/sunset helps you plan around light.
Daily score hours
Daily scores can be calculated using all hours or daylight only.

The week view and day score follow your Settings preference. The day page also lets you toggle the view locally while you’re looking at a day.
Tides
Where available, Skippy shows modelled tide events (high/low) based on the underlying sea level curve.

Treat this as a handy guide rather than a harbour-master source.
Mooring access
For some locations, access to a mooring is limited around high or low water.

In Settings, you can tell Skippy how long either side of high water and low water your mooring cannot be accessed. These limits are optional and can be set independently.

On the Day view, Skippy shows these periods next to the tide times, labelled as (High) or (Low).

This feature is intended as a planning aid only. It does not affect boating scores, safety gates, or recommended windows.
Data & privacy
Skippy uses the Open-Meteo weather + marine models. There are no accounts and no tracking built into the app.

Your settings are stored locally in your browser.
Safety notice
Skippy provides guidance only. Always check official marine forecasts, carry appropriate safety equipment, and plan conservatively. Conditions can change rapidly.
